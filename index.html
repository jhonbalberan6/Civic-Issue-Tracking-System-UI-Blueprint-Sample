04 / Core Technical Pillars & Pitch Differentiation

Why CivicSync Wins LGU Buy-In: Static Focus & Vendor Accountability

🎯

"Static" Over "Transient" Focus

Skips fleeting issues (double-parked cars) for static hazards (potholes, clogged drains, spaghetti wires) that stay put—guaranteeing clean 24–48h Resolved metrics.

🧾

Vendor Accountability Engine

Audits third-party contractors (garbage haulers, road crews, telcos) via SLA timers & photo proof—saving City Hall staff work instead of adding to it.

📡

Typhoon-Ready Offline Sync

Built for Cagayan Valley brownouts & dead zones: IndexedDB + WebP compression queues citizen & field-crew photos until signal returns.

📊

Archived Hotspot Mining

Turns Archived closure logs into Spatial Hotspot Maps & CSV/GeoJSON Exports so analysts spot repeat 30m clusters immediately.

📸

Before/After Proof Gate

Contractors cannot close a ticket or claim SLA credit without a geotagged "After" Photo broadcast to all subscribed citizens.
