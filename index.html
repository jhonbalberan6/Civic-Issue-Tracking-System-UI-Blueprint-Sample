04 / Capstone & LGU Pitch Defense

Solving Fragmented Data in Philippine Local Systems

At its core, CivicSync targets the root bottleneck in Philippine governance—fragmented, siloed civic data—by unifying offline citizen reports, LGU department routing, and photo-verified records into one interoperable platform.

🔗
Core Thesis • Root-Cause Intervention

From Scattered Silos to One Standardized Civic Record

In many Philippine LGUs, citizen complaints are fragmented across personal Facebook posts, barangay logbooks, SMS texts, and disconnected department spreadsheets—causing lost tickets, duplicate dispatches, and zero historical memory. CivicSync creates a single, offline-resilient data pipeline from barangay street level to city-wide spatial analytics.

01 • DIFFERENTIATION LGU-Native Engineering

1. What makes this unique?

  • 🏛️
    Built around a specific LGU's real workflow: Commercial or national apps use generic ticket categories. CivicSync maps directly to Tuguegarao's partner barangay & city office hierarchy (City Engineering, GSO, POSU, CAGELCO I), escalation rules, and approval steps.
  • ⚡
    Intelligent triage, not just a digital dropbox: Instead of simply collecting reports, our ingestion layer uses geospatial duplicate clustering (30m radius), geofenced auto-routing, and static-issue urgency scoring so staff aren't flooded with redundant tickets.
  • 📡
    Designed for local constraints: Engineered specifically for Philippine connectivity and demographics—combining offline-first IndexedDB caching, 350KB client-side WebP image compression, and zero-install QR/browser access.
02 • COMPETITIVE EDGE vs. Facebook / National Apps

2. Why use this specifically over existing apps?

  • 🔒
    Data ownership and privacy: LGUs cannot easily audit, control, or export custom spatial datasets from third-party public apps or messy Facebook group chats. CivicSync gives the LGU full database ownership and one-click CSV/GeoJSON reporting.
  • 📲
    Zero adoption barrier for residents: Residents rarely download a dedicated government app just to report one broken streetlight. A browser-based Progressive Web App (PWA) or barangay QR scan lets citizens report instantly without installing anything.
  • 📸
    Closed-loop accountability: Existing channels leave citizens wondering if anyone saw their post. CivicSync enforces transparent lifecycle tracking (Submitted → Verified → Assigned → Resolved with "After" photo proof) and SLA turnaround timers.
03 • PROBLEM MOTIVATION Stakeholder-Driven

3. Why did you pick this topic?

  • 🤝
    Validated problem from a real stakeholder: Grounded in the root reality of fragmented data across Philippine local offices—where lost paper blotters, unorganized Messenger inquiries, and siloed barangay records stall manual dispatching—and engineered to unify them.
  • 🛠️
    High social impact paired with full-stack complexity: Enables our team to demonstrate end-to-end software engineering—offline IndexedDB sync, GIS geospatial clustering, role-based access control, SLA state machines, and spatial analytics—while building a tool Tuguegarao communities can actually deploy.
04 • CAPSTONE CONTRIBUTION Applied Engineering & Evaluation

4. Where is the originality?

  • 🧩
    Applied adaptation over pure invention: In IT capstones, originality comes from synthesizing and adapting technologies to a localized context. We combine proven patterns—GIS mapping, offline PWA queues, automated routing, and photo-verified state gates—engineered specifically for Philippine LGU realities.
  • 📊
    Empirical evaluation in a real environment: Our original research contribution includes measurable field benchmarks—comparing ticket routing speed, duplicate reduction rates, and System Usability Scale (SUS) scores among local officials and residents.
